### 2.2 Lead-gen form patterns that convert

**Universal skeleton (SmartAsset, Unbiased, WiserAdvisor, Paladin, LendingTree, Credible): 3 visible steps**
1. *Answer a few questions* (framed as "2-minute quiz" / "~5 minutes")
2. *Get matched* (algorithm / "up to 3" / "2 to 3 advisors near you")
3. *Free, no-obligation intro call* (Unbiased: within 24h / 1 business day)

**Entry hook (first screen must be one tap):**
- State / US map picker (SmartAsset, WiserAdvisor), ZIP (Paladin, NAPFA), advisor-type selector (Unbiased), "top financial priority" tiles (NerdWallet), scenario buttons (Ratehub).

**Question order (from SmartAsset per RetirementLiving + observed patterns), one question per screen, progress bar:**
1. Goal / help needed (retirement, investing, tax, estate, debt, "not sure")
2. Location (state/province, ZIP/postal code)
3. Age band / retirement timeline
4. Investable assets (bands: <$100K, $100–250K, $250–500K, $500K–1M, $1M+); **this is the lead-price driver**
5. Annual income band
6. Marital status / dependants
7. Risk tolerance
8. Preferred advisor style (fee-only fiduciary, in-person vs virtual)
9. Contact: first name → email → phone (phone last, with explicit consent checkbox)

SmartAsset runs about 20 questions; a lean Private.Finance version should run 7–9. Lead value scales with investable assets. Mass-affluent and HNW ($250K+) are the target, which fits "private wealth".

**Trust copy that recurs:**
- "Free. No obligation to hire." / "No Match Fee." (WiserAdvisor)
- "Your information is protected / safe and secure / confidential and encrypted"
- "We only share your information with the advisors you're matched with" (WiserAdvisor)
- "Vetted / pre-screened fiduciaries", "FCA-regulated and qualified"
- "Won't affect your credit score" (Credible, lending flows)
- Network size ("27,000 qualified professionals"), founding year, press logos, Trustpilot score
- Compliance footer: referral service, compensated by advisors, does not give advice or manage assets

**CTA wording that recurs:** "Take Matching Quiz", "Get matched now", "Match with an adviser for free", "Find an Advisor", "Get Started", "Compare advisors", "Ready for the next step?" (post-calculator), "Am I Ready for an Advisor?" (quiz-as-CTA). Exit-intent popup (WiserAdvisor).

**Secondary capture:** free directory / interview guide (Paladin "Download Your FREE Copy"), "Questions to ask an advisor" checklist PDF, email-course boot camp (WCI).

**Privacy-first twist for Private.Finance:** keep calculators fully client-side with no sign-up; state it ("Your numbers never leave your browser"). Ask for contact details only at the final step. Keep the explicit consent line and a "no selling of data" statement. Competitors already lead with this copy, so for this brand it should be the main pitch.

### 2.3 Content and trust patterns for YMYL + AdSense approval

Required pages and sitewide elements:
- **About** (who runs it, mission, why privacy-first), **Editorial Policy / Standards**, **How We Make Money / Advertiser Disclosure**, **Privacy Policy** (AdSense needs it, plus cookie/consent and GDPR/CCPA "Do Not Sell"), **Terms**, **Contact** (real email/address), **Corrections policy**, **Methodology** (for calculators and any ratings).
- **Per-article byline block:** Written by (bio link) → Reviewed by (credentialed: CFP®, CFA, CPA, CEPF®) → Edited by; "Fact checked" badge; **"Updated [date]"** + read time (NerdWallet format: "Updated Oct 26, 2026 · 5 min read").
- **Author pages** with credentials, specialties and links to other work (NerdWallet, Kiplinger "Meet our experts", Forbes "Our Editorial Team").
- **Disclosure placement:** short advertiser disclosure link above the H1 (Bankrate, Forbes); educational-only and "not investment advice" line under the byline (NerdWallet); full compensation disclosure in the footer.
- **Sources:** in-text links to primary sources (SEC, FINRA, CFPB, IRS, CRA, FCA) and a numbered source list at the end (SmartAsset).
- **Calculator pages:** an assumptions/methodology block, a "hypothetical, not a guarantee" disclaimer, plus FAQs.
- **Sponsored labeling:** "Sponsored" / "Partner" tags on any paid placement (Kiplinger, Ratehub, MoneySense).
- **Lead-gen compliance footer** (SmartAsset/WiserAdvisor/Paladin model): the site is a referral service, may be compensated by advisors, does not provide advice or manage assets; tell users to review Form ADV/CRS (US) or the regulator register.
- **Weak examples to avoid:** Moneyunder30 (no bylines or disclosures visible) and Omni (no reviewer credentials). Both get by on domain age that a new site does not have.
- Practical AdSense notes: launch with about 25–40 substantive pages; avoid thin calculator-only pages; no pop-ups that block content before approval; real contact info; consistent dates.

### 2.4 Design / UX patterns

- **Nav:** mega-menu by money goal or product (NerdWallet 9 categories; Millennial Money by goal: Make/Save/Invest/Borrow/Plan). Recommended for Private.Finance: **Tools · Guides · Private Wealth · Protect (privacy/fraud) · Find an Advisor**, plus search. A persistent "Find an Advisor" button in the header (SmartAsset dual CTA; Ramsey "Get Started").
- **Hero formulas that work:**
  - Stat/problem hook: Bankrate "9 out of 10 homebuyers overpay…" + 3 stats.
  - Promise + single CTA: SmartAsset "Get Clarity. Find Your Advisor." + rotating sample questions.
  - Goal selector tiles under the hero (YNAB, Millennial Money, NerdWallet).
  - Trust strip directly under the hero (press logos / stats / "since").
- **Calculator page layout (best-of-breed composite):**
  1. Breadcrumb → disclosure link → H1 + one-line subhead
  2. Two-column widget: inputs left (sliders + number fields, defaults pre-filled); results right (big headline number, chart, 3 summary figures); live update without a Calculate button (Bankrate updates live, SmartAsset uses a button)
  3. **Advisor CTA directly under results**, phrased as "Ready for the next step?"
  4. Tabs: chart / year-by-year table / amortization
  5. "How this calculator works" assumptions
  6. Long explainer with formula, worked example and "what-if" scenarios (Bankrate, Calculator.net)
  7. FAQs → sources → byline/reviewer block → related-calculator grid → newsletter
- **Article layout:** breadcrumbs → H1 → updated/read-time → byline trio → disclosure → TOC → key-takeaways box → body with callouts ("Want a shortcut?") → FAQ → related-article cards.
- **Visual style:** clean card-based layouts with lots of white space (Wealthsimple, Monarch); restrained palette with one accent (Unbiased teal, Groww purple); app/product mockups; line-art illustration (YNAB); data-dense tables only where needed (Morningstar).
- **Mobile:** sticky bottom CTA bar (ClearTax) for "Find an Advisor".

### 2.5 Top 10 highest-value content topics (traffic × RPM × lead intent)

1. **Financial advisor fees & "Is a financial advisor worth it?"** (AUM % vs flat vs hourly; SmartAsset frames 0.59–1.18% AUM, $7.5K flat, $120–300/hr) + advisor fee calculator. Highest lead intent.
2. **How to choose / find a fee-only fiduciary advisor** (questions to ask, how to check BrokerCheck/IAPD/CIRO/FCA register). Matches NerdWallet's top advisor article.
3. **Retirement calculator / "How much do I need to retire" / 4% rule & FIRE number**. Evergreen, high RPM, feeds the advisor funnel.
4. **Compound interest / investment growth calculator**. Present on every site in this set; top traffic.
5. **Mortgage calculator + PMI / affordability / amortization**. Highest-RPM consumer vertical (Bankrate, Ratehub, Money.com).
6. **Private wealth & private banking explained** (what private banks offer, minimums, wealth management vs private banking vs family office). This is the brand's niche, and competitors cover it thinly.
7. **Private equity & private credit for individual investors** (interval funds, accredited investor rules, fees, risks). Fast-growing topic in 2025–26 news and a magnet for HNW leads.
8. **Debt payoff (snowball vs avalanche) calculator + emergency fund calculator**. Mass traffic, strong engagement (Ramsey, NerdWallet).
9. **Financial privacy & identity theft protection** (credit freeze vs lock, data brokers, scam red flags). This is the brand's differentiator; Ramsey, investor.gov and WalletHub all invest in it.
10. **Budgeting (50/30/20) + net worth tracker (private, no sign-up)**. Huge volume; the privacy angle stands out against Monarch and YNAB, which require sign-up.
