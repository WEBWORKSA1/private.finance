Advisor matching FAQ

Is this really free?

Yes. There is no fee to be matched and no obligation to hire anyone. Advisors you choose to work with will explain their own fees before you commit.

Who will see my information?

Only our team and the advisor(s) we introduce you to. We do not sell your details to data brokers or mass-marketing lists. See our privacy policy.

How does Private.Finance make money from this?

We may receive a referral fee from professional firms when we introduce clients. That never changes what you pay, and we prioritise fee-only, fiduciary-minded professionals.

What should I ask on the first call?

Ask whether they are a fiduciary at all times, how they are paid, their all-in cost, who holds your assets and what a typical client looks like. Our fee-only advisor guide has a full checklist.
