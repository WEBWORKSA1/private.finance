Trusted financial resources by country

Regulators, government guidance and official registers we rely on in our research — including where to verify an advisor before you hire one.

United States

Consumer Financial Protection Bureau ↗

Complaints, guides and tools on credit, mortgages and debt.

Investor.gov (SEC) ↗

Investor education and the SEC's adviser search.

FINRA BrokerCheck ↗

Check a broker's or firm's registration and disciplinary history.

SEC Investment Adviser Search ↗

Look up registered investment advisers and their Form ADV.

IdentityTheft.gov (FTC) ↗

Report identity theft and get a recovery plan.

AnnualCreditReport.com ↗

The official site for free credit reports.

CFP Board — Let's Make a Plan ↗

Find and verify CFP® professionals.

Canada

Financial Consumer Agency of Canada ↗

Banking, credit and budgeting guidance.

CIRO ↗

Canadian Investment Regulatory Organization — check advisors.

Canadian Securities Administrators ↗

National registration search for advisers and dealers.

United Kingdom

MoneyHelper ↗

Free, impartial money and pensions guidance.

FCA Financial Services Register ↗

Check that a firm or adviser is authorised.

India

SEBI ↗

Securities regulator — verify registered investment advisers.

Reserve Bank of India ↗

Banking regulation and consumer awareness.

Australia

Moneysmart (ASIC) ↗

Free calculators and guidance from the regulator.

ASIC Professional Register ↗

Check financial advisers and licensees.

Global

OECD Financial Education ↗

International research on financial literacy.
