Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. "Local storage" is a similar browser feature. We use both sparingly.

What we store

NamePurposeType
pf_cookie_okRemembers your cookie-notice choiceEssential (local storage)
pf_themeRemembers light/dark modePreference (local storage)
pf_currencyRemembers your calculator currencyPreference (local storage)
pf_networth, pf_health_scoreOnly if you choose to save them — stays on your devicePreference (local storage)
Google AdSense cookiesServe, personalise and measure ads; fraud preventionAdvertising (third party)

Third-party cookies

Google and its partners may set cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. See our privacy policy for opt-out links. YouTube may set cookies only after you play an embedded video.

Your control

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, choose "Essential only" in our notice, and manage ad personalisation at Google Ads Settings.
